Pepsi

Brickasso® No. 02, a one of one artwork about movement, memory and the life between Italy and Romania.

Brickasso® No. 02

A child between two worlds.

Pepsi is Brickasso® No. 02, an original one of one artwork built from 8,750 original LEGO® elements. The work uses the form of a watch to speak about time, movement and identity.

The story belongs to Albert. He was born in Italy and lives in Romania. Inside the claw of the watch, red carries Italy and blue carries Romania. The two colors hold the tension between the place where life began and the place where life continues.

From a distance, the work reads as a bold object. Up close, it becomes a small world in motion, with dozens of minifigures caught inside the mechanism of time.

Story

Italy, Romania and the mechanism of life.

The red side of the claw refers to Italy. The blue side refers to Romania. Between them, the watch becomes a symbol for a childhood shaped by two places, two memories and one continuous movement.

Dozens of minifigures appear inside the claw like people moving through life. They create activity, direction and noise around the central form.

At the center sits the factory of life. It suggests the place where time, people, memory and personal identity are constantly assembled.

Concept

A watch that holds memory.

Pepsi starts from a familiar visual object, but the work does not remain only a watch. It becomes a structure for Albert's own story, born in Italy and living in Romania.

The artwork connects time with origin. The hands, the claw, the colors and the internal scenes build a personal map, where each detail adds another layer to the image.

Artistic Significance

The second chapter.

As BRK-02, Pepsi follows The White Horse and expands the Brickasso® language toward narrative detail, symbolic color and internal scenes.

The work records an early moment in the catalogue where the visible image and the hidden story begin to work together as one object.

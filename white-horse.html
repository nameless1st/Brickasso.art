Brickasso® Registry, BRK-01

First Official Work

The White Horse | Brickasso® No. 01

The White Horse is the first official work in the Brickasso® Registry and marks the beginning of Albert Oprea's artistic catalogue. Created from 18,322 original LEGO® elements, it established the visual language, sculptural depth and manual building process that continue to define every Brickasso® artwork.

The composition is defined by clarity, restraint and presence. The horse appears calm from a distance, but the surface reveals thousands of precise placements when viewed up close. Light, relief and rhythm work together to transform individual elements into a single sculptural image.

Set against a Novacolor ARCHI+ Big background and held within a solid wood frame, The White Horse connects material discipline with symbolic force. It is the work from which the official Brickasso® identity begins.

The Story

The White Horse was created at the moment when Brickasso® moved from experiment to artistic identity. It transformed a personal way of building into an official body of work, signed, recorded and presented as contemporary art.

The horse was chosen for its association with strength, freedom and trust. In this work, those ideas are expressed through balance rather than force. The image does not depend on excess. It depends on control, rhythm and the quiet tension of the form.

Historical Position

Every artistic body of work has a beginning. Within the Brickasso® catalogue, The White Horse occupies that position. As BRK-01, it serves as the reference point from which Albert Oprea's visual language, construction techniques and long term artistic vision developed.

This position gives the work a permanent role inside the official Brickasso® Registry. It will always remain the first signed artwork in the catalogue.

Artistic Significance

The White Horse defines several elements that became central to Brickasso®: large scale execution, layered relief, original LEGO® elements as a serious medium, and the ability to create an image that changes between distance and close viewing.

It also established the relationship between image and object. The work is not only seen. It occupies space, carries texture and records the physical time invested in its construction.

Collectibility

Collectibility is shaped by originality, historical relevance, documented provenance and limited production. As the first registered Brickasso® artwork, The White Horse occupies a unique position within the artist's catalogue and will permanently remain BRK-01.

Its importance is connected to its role as a foundation work, not to public price display. Collector discussions are handled privately through official contact channels.

BRK Registry and Provenance

The White Horse is recorded in the Brickasso® Registry as BRK-01. The registry preserves the artwork's identity, provenance and place inside the official catalogue. The physical certificate includes a private verification code reserved for the owner.

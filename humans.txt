/* ARTISTIC PRACTICE */
Registered Brickasso® artworks are original one of one physical artworks built through LEGO® elements.
Selected artworks may also be released separately as signed and numbered Fine Art editions with separate edition documentation.
Fine Art editions do not replace or inherit the BRK Registry identity of the original physical artwork.
The website documents concept, chronology, authenticity, provenance context, hidden narratives, sculptural relief and artistic evolution.

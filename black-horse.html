Brickasso® Registry: BRK-04

The Black Horse | Brickasso® No. 04

The Black Horse is Brickasso® No. 04, a one of one sculptural LEGO® artwork by Albert Oprea. Built from 17,622 original LEGO® elements, it extends the equine language that began with The White Horse and brings it into a darker, more dramatic register of motion, contrast and relief.

The Story

The Black Horse continues a visual path opened by BRK-01, but it does not repeat it. Where The White Horse established the beginning, The Black Horse pushes the language forward through stronger contrast, a more dynamic silhouette and a deeper relationship between figure and surface.

The subject carries tension and movement. From a distance, the horse appears as a powerful shape emerging from the background. Up close, the surface reveals thousands of controlled placements that build rhythm, muscle, shadow and direction.

Historical Position

Within the Brickasso® catalogue, The Black Horse marks an important development after the first official work. It shows how the artist returned to the equine theme with greater confidence, more structural control and a stronger sense of sculptural presence.

As BRK-04, the artwork documents a key stage in the evolution of the Brickasso® method. It stands as a bridge between the foundational language of The White Horse and the larger narrative works that followed.

Artistic Significance

The composition is built around movement. The figure is defined by diagonal energy, layered relief and carefully balanced negative space. The dark palette gives the work a different emotional weight, while the LEGO® structure creates texture that changes with light and viewing distance.

The Black Horse demonstrates how Brickasso® can turn the same subject family into a new visual experience. It is not a second version of The White Horse. It is an evolution in contrast, mass, atmosphere and control.

Collectibility

Collectibility is shaped by originality, documented provenance, technical maturity and the position of a work within the artist's catalogue. The Black Horse holds a strong place in the Brickasso® Registry because it records the evolution of a key subject and the refinement of a visual language first introduced through BRK-01.

Its importance comes from contrast, scale, manual execution and its role as an early signed work in the official Brickasso® body of work.

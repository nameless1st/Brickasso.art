The Champion's Blade | Brickasso® No. 13

The Champion's Blade is Brickasso® No. 13, a one-of-one LEGO® 3D mosaic created as a tribute to Olympic champion Ana Maria Brânză. The work reinterprets the fencing épée as a symbol of discipline, precision and quiet strength.

Built by hand from 1,000 original LEGO® elements, the composition transforms a sporting object into a sculptural sign of dedication. From a distance, the elegant line of the blade feels direct and clear. Up close, the viewer discovers the patient construction behind it, piece by piece.

The artwork was completed in Cluj-Napoca on 13 June 2026 and recorded as BRK-13 in the Brickasso® Registry. Although intimate in scale, it carries the same artistic language as the larger Brickasso® works: structure, emotion, detail and a story that remains beyond the moment.

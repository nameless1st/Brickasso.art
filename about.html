About the Artist

Albert Oprea, Brickasso®

Albert Oprea, known as Brickasso®, is the artist behind a contemporary practice dedicated to original one of one sculptural artworks created from original LEGO® elements. His work combines manual construction, layered relief, visual storytelling and museum scale ambition.

The Story Behind Brickasso®

Albert began building with LEGO® at the age of four. Over time, loose bricks replaced instructions, and construction became creation. What started as play developed into a personal visual language based on patience, structure and imagination.

Brickasso® is the name of that artistic language. It transforms thousands of individual elements into coherent images that can be read from a distance and rediscovered up close. The surface carries color, shadow, relief and hidden details, turning each work into both image and object.

The White Horse marked a defining moment in this journey. It became the first official signed work, recorded as number 01, and established the foundation of the Brickasso® identity.

Artistic Practice

Brickasso® does not rely on automated mosaic generators or digital conversion software to define the final artwork. Each composition is built through manual decisions, material testing, sorting, placement and adjustment.

Every LEGO® element functions as a unit of color, shadow and structure. From far away, the image becomes clear. From close range, the viewer discovers micro scenes, relief changes and the physical evidence of the time invested in the work.

Artistic Vision

Brickasso® is not about using LEGO® as decoration. It is about transforming a familiar material into a serious artistic medium. The goal is to build works that carry presence, memory and long term meaning.

Each artwork is treated as a permanent object, not as a temporary construction. The practice stands between contemporary art, sculpture, architecture and visual storytelling.

Recognition

Artistic Values

The Brickasso® practice is built around a clear set of artistic values. These values guide the creation, documentation and presentation of each official work.

Originality

Each work is conceived as an original composition with its own identity, story and place in the Brickasso® catalogue.

Manual Construction

The artwork is built by hand, piece by piece, with decisions made throughout the process.

One of One

Official Brickasso® works are unique originals. The same artwork is not reproduced as an edition.

Scale

Many works are created at large scale, where size, relief and distance change the viewer's experience.

Documentation

Each signed work is recorded, documented and connected to the BRK Registry and certificate system.

Long Term Vision

The body of work is developed as a serious artistic catalogue intended to grow over time.

One of One Philosophy

A Brickasso® artwork is not produced as a repeated edition. Each official work is created as a unique object with its own composition, structure, timeline and documentation.

This one of one approach protects the identity of each piece and gives every work a fixed place within the artist's developing body of work.

BRK Registry and Authenticity

Official Brickasso® artworks are recorded in the BRK Registry. Collectors receive a signed Certificate of Authenticity with private code and QR verification through the official Brickasso® website.

The registry preserves provenance, confirms authenticity and connects each work to its place in the official Brickasso® catalogue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Albert Oprea?

Albert Oprea is the Romanian artist behind Brickasso®, a contemporary practice focused on original one of one sculptural artworks created from LEGO® elements.

What is Brickasso®?

Brickasso® is the artistic identity and body of work created by Albert Oprea. It transforms LEGO® elements into signed, registered and certificate verified artworks.

What makes Brickasso® different?

Brickasso® combines manual construction, scale, relief, hidden details, narrative composition and BRK Registry documentation. The works are created as unique originals.

Are Brickasso® artworks one of one?

Yes. Official Brickasso® artworks are created as one of one originals. They are not reproduced as editions.

How is authenticity verified?

Each official work is connected to the BRK Registry and accompanied by a signed Certificate of Authenticity with private code and QR verification.

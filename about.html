About the Artist

Albert Oprea

The artist behind Brickasso®, a story shaped by curiosity, patience and the quiet need to build what does not yet exist.

A personal beginning

Before Brickasso® had a name, Albert was already building a way of seeing.

His story did not begin with a finished artwork. It began with a child returning to the same pieces again and again, trying to understand how something simple could become something new.

The beginning

Albert's story did not begin with an artwork. It began with curiosity.

As a young child, he spent hours building with LEGO® elements, following the instructions with patience and precision. Cars, houses, vehicles and entire miniature worlds appeared one after another. As his confidence grew, so did the complexity of the sets. By the age of six, he was already building models designed for adults.

What fascinated him most was never the finished model. It was understanding how thousands of individual elements could become one coherent idea.

Without realizing it, he was already developing the way of thinking that would later become Brickasso®.

Beyond instructions

After completing each set, Albert often took it apart and rebuilt it without instructions.

That simple habit changed everything. The pieces were no longer limited to a single model. The same elements could become countless different forms, depending only on composition, proportion and imagination.

For the first time, he stopped seeing individual pieces and began seeing possibilities.

LEGO® is more than a toy. It is how I build my own path.

Albert Oprea

Building was no longer about following instructions. It had become a way of observing, solving problems and expressing ideas. Without knowing it, Albert had already begun developing his own visual language.

The first image

After years of building objects, Albert created something different. His first image was a clock.

It was a small work, yet it marked an important turning point. For the first time, LEGO® elements were no longer used to build an object. They were used to create an image.

That realization changed the direction of his work. An image could carry composition, rhythm, balance and emotion in ways a model could not.

From that moment, every new attempt brought him closer to a visual language that would eventually become Brickasso®.

The first official work

At the age of ten, Albert created The White Horse, the first official Brickasso® artwork, documented as BRK-01.

It marked the moment when years of curiosity, experimentation and quiet persistence came together in a single work. The White Horse did not appear suddenly. It grew naturally from everything that came before it.

During this period, Albert was also guided by Gerardo Pontierr, whose advice helped him better understand important artistic and technical aspects of his work. Albert remains deeply grateful for that guidance.

With The White Horse, Brickasso® was no longer just an idea. It became the beginning of an artistic practice that continues to evolve, one artwork at a time.

Still building

Albert continues to build with the same curiosity and patience that shaped his earliest years.

Every artwork begins long before the first piece is placed. It starts with an idea, grows through observation and develops through hundreds of careful decisions. Every detail has a purpose, including those that remain hidden from the first view.

I never give up, no matter how difficult it becomes.

Albert Oprea

Today, Brickasso® continues to grow one artwork at a time. Each new work becomes another chapter in an artistic practice that is still being written.

Public record

As the body of work continued to grow, Brickasso® gradually became part of the public record.

The artworks have been presented through exhibitions, cultural collaborations, media features and national television, including Albert's appearance as a finalist on national television in 2026.

Each appearance documents another stage in the evolution of the artistic practice. Together, they create a public history that exists alongside the artworks themselves, preserving where they were seen, how they were presented and how the Brickasso® catalogue continues to grow.

The artistic practice

Brickasso® is Albert Oprea's personal artistic practice. LEGO® elements are the material, while every artwork begins with an idea, develops through composition and reveals additional narratives beyond the visible image.

One of one works

Each finished Brickasso® artwork is original and one of one. The work is built by hand and documented within the permanent Brickasso® Catalogue.

The BRK catalogue

Every official artwork receives a permanent BRK number. Each BRK number permanently documents one chapter in Albert's artistic evolution, from The White Horse, BRK-01, to the works that followed.

Hidden narratives

The visible image is only the first layer. Many works contain hidden scenes, relief details, symbols and personal references that give the artwork a second reading.

Every artwork begins with the same curiositythat started years ago.
